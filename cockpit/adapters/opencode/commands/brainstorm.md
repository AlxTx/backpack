Brainstorm this question or opportunity: $ARGUMENTS

Use relevant conversation and project evidence, but remain strictly read-only.
Open the solution space before converging:

1. Restate the objective and material constraints.
2. Generate 3 genuinely different options, including the simplest viable one.
3. Compare benefits, costs, risks, reversibility, and unknowns.
4. Challenge the leading assumption.
5. Recommend one direction and explain why.

Do not implement and do not manufacture an execution plan unless explicitly
requested. End with the smallest decision or next experiment needed.
