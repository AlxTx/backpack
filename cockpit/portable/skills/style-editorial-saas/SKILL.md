# Style Pack: Editorial SaaS

Use when the product needs a stronger point of view: landing pages, onboarding,
AI tools, creative tools, or product surfaces that benefit from storytelling.

## Direction

- Mood: editorial, confident, designed, memorable.
- Layout: asymmetric grids, magazine-like sections, large type, deliberate
  contrast between dense and quiet zones.
- Typography: expressive display headings balanced with readable body text.
- Color: one dominant atmospheric palette with sharp accent moments.
- Details: art-directed cards, contextual backgrounds, tasteful illustration or
  abstract geometry.

## Avoid

- Generic hero + feature cards.
- Decorative complexity that hides the product action.
- Copying a famous brand's exact palette or signature motifs.
