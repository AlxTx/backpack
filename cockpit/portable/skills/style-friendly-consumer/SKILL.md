# Style Pack: Friendly Consumer

Use for onboarding, collaboration, customer support, marketplaces, consumer
workflows, or tools that must feel warm and low-friction.

## Direction

- Mood: warm, approachable, trustworthy, human.
- Layout: simple flows, clear cards, helpful empty states, gentle guidance.
- Typography: readable, friendly, not childish.
- Color: warm neutrals, friendly accent, clear success/error semantics.
- Details: soft illustrations or icons, rounded but controlled surfaces,
  supportive microcopy.

## Avoid

- Toy-like visuals for serious workflows.
- Too many colors competing with the primary action.
- Decorative friendliness that reduces efficiency.
